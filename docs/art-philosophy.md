# Lattice Calm

A visual movement for the artwork in `public/art`. Rendered by
`scripts/generate-art.py`.

Lattice Calm holds that structure is a kind of quiet. Each surface begins as a
deep slate ground, nearly black at the edges and faintly lit from within, and is
filled by patient accumulation: hundreds of hairlines, points and rings, each one
placed by a rule and none placed by hand. No single mark matters. The field
matters. The work should read as the product of long, meticulous labour, the kind
of plate a master draughtsman would spend weeks ruling.

Colour is rationed to three voices. Slate carries the space. Off-white carries the
mass of marks at low opacity, so density reads as light. A single cyan carries
attention and appears in less than a tenth of the marks: one path through a
network, one stream in a current, the crest of a column. Where cyan appears, it
is the answer the rest of the field was computing.

Form comes from repetition under a rule: a line integrated through a field, a
rectangle divided until it cannot be divided, a chord thrown across an ellipse
seven hundred times until a hollow appears at the centre. The diagonal of the
folded ribbon recurs as the governing angle. Compositions are asymmetric, weighted
to one side, with generous untouched ground left for the marks to breathe.

Scale works at two distances. From across a room the piece is one calm shape. At
arm's length it resolves into fine, exact detail with no blur, no stray pixel and
no mark touching the edge. Every margin is deliberate. Every alignment is the
result of painstaking calibration, the mark of deep expertise applied without
hurry.

There is no text. The image does not explain itself. It is an instrument reading
from an imaginary discipline, executed with master-level care, in which something
invisible has been measured and found to be orderly.
